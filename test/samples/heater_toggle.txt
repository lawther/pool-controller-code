# Heater toggle - CMD 0x1A (Heater Toggle Command), sent to All Heaters
# (0x007F).
#
# Captured 2026-10-25 on lawther's install: a Connect 10 with an ICI gas heater
# (0x0074), pump and chlorinator unplugged, so the heater could switch on but
# never fire (status "No Flow"). Every 0x1A here was injected over the TCP
# bridge as the Touchscreen (0x0050), on its own.
#
# The heater answers each toggle with its own CMD 0x12 status about 0.4-0.5 s
# later. Other frames injected in the same session, and idle broadcast traffic,
# have been dropped.
